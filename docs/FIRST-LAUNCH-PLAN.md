## Proposed first-release promise

A designer can set up a supported WordPress/WooCommerce site on shared hosting, collect the client's business context, deliver a Bricks website through Seam, and hand over an understandable mobile-friendly workspace. Clients manage connected business details, selected design resources, team, content and navigation in their proper native records. Visitors get dependable DSA account and PWA journeys. Optional AI and notifications have explicit configuration, permissions and spending/delivery boundaries. The install can be updated and recovered using a documented, tested process.

This is the lead engineer's proposed launch boundary based on the standing vision. It does not mark unfinished features as delivered or erase the longer roadmap. The supported configuration and limitations must be published with the release.
