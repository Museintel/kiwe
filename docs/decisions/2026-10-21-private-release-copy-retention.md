# Retire obsolete private release copies after verified recovery

Status: accepted operational policy, 21 October 2026.

Old operator plugin copies and private restore rehearsals accumulated beside the websites. Their file count exhausted the shared account even while disk space remained available, blocking the compiler's native deployment. The founder explicitly requests clearing unnecessary backups on Dharwad, National and the lab.

Keep an immediate rollback for the active release and required current recovery inputs. After a work unit closes, consolidate superseded operator copies into authenticated, independently verified offline custody, then retire their exact unchanged server trees. Reuse identical objects and already authenticated source members where digest and size match. Preserve original paths and recovery metadata. Remove failed derived ZIPs and temporary transport copies after the accepted independent recovery is secured; leaving large new operator archives on hosting is not cleanup completion.

Native client-managed backups retain their existing service and retention authority. Do not infer that every folder outside `public_html` is disposable, delete unlisted account/configuration data, clear client backup history as a test, or count free filesystem bytes as account inode headroom. Record fresh provider capacity after cleanup and before another substantial restore or build.

Use exact owned paths, no symlink traversal, bounded capture, original failure receipts, pre-delete full-tree checks and per-file stale guards. Public application roots and commerce databases are outside the operator-copy deletion scope. Offline capture/readback does not establish full-site metadata, provider replay or running-destination recovery acceptance. See [the CL105 scope and evidence](../HOSTING-PRIVATE-COPY-CLEANUP-2026-10-21.md).
