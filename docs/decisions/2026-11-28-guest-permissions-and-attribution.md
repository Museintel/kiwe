# Guest permissions and attribution

The Guest App owns the application and its approved permissions. Profile DSA offers the contribution types enabled on the site. Guest managers approve the requested permissions separately. Product proposals and articles use the existing protected WordPress contributor role and editor review. Referral-only approval keeps the person's existing customer or subscriber role. An existing approval with no newer grant field keeps its original article or product permission. Product proposals require Store. Referrals can credit account registrations with Store off.

The Links and Profile DSA use the existing public subject resolver for referral destinations. The private Share response issues a site-bound link to a currently approved, verified Guest. The public landing asks the visitor whether to credit the referral. The first accepted link is remembered in a signed, expiring cookie for up to 30 days. A new account created during that period can carry the referral to subsequent orders. An approved referrer’s own account and orders cannot generate referral credit for them. Revocation and suspension stop new credit. An attributed order keeps its original record for reconciliation.

**Analytics → Guest** and the read-only MCP report show new accounts attributed to the selected Guest. Order detail additionally requires WooCommerce order capabilities, including permission on the individual order. Reports exclude unpaid checkout attempts and show WooCommerce refunds on paid orders. Sales of an approved Guest product are attributed to the author at checkout and shown by item, including when someone else supplied the referral. A Guest report shows the latest 20 matching orders per page and up to three contributed product lines per order. No payment data or customer contact details are published. Existing WooCommerce coupons set the discount. Kiwe Launch’s Pass, billing, referral commissions and withdrawals belong to its own service.

The physical release requires matching Core, Guest and MCP packages. Refer to the [maintainer contract](../MAINTAINER-CONTRACT.md) for signing and recovery. Qualify the public consent redirect, Link and Profile DSA, and the installed App combination on the hosted rehearsal before fleet deployment.
