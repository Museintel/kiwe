## What changed

- Pages expose Allow search indexing, Main menu and Visitor DSA menu on the card. Indexing intent is independent of navigation. The card reports when site-wide indexing is off or a page is still a draft; it never promises a search ranking or crawler inclusion. New draft pages start with indexing off. Draft pages/services cannot be newly placed in a menu, and unpublished destinations are omitted from both menu projections.
- Categories expose indexing on the card as well as the existing menu switches. Post, product and service categories have separate filters and matching record counts. Creating, assigning, renaming and moving selected services now use category IDs instead of the sample's old free-text category string. Service names/categories project into navigation without copying their labels. Services also have menu switches on their cards.
- Navigation shows Main menu and Visitor DSA menu previews from the same source-linked selections. Existing label overrides retain their meaning. Empty category searches explain how to continue.
- Notifications is accessible from the global bell and a Home tile. It has unread/all views, search, mark read/unread, mark all read, related-work links and a Preferences view. Bell, Home and list share the same sample read state. Marking an update read does not change its underlying comment, article or order. The bell respects unsaved editing.
- Delivery tiles show Browser/PWA, Email, WhatsApp and unavailable SMS. Role-relevant topic tiles show editorial, commerce, guest-contribution and security events. Visitor telemetry topics remain unavailable in this synthetic site profile. Personal delivery preferences do not control authentication, passkeys, PhoneKey or global telemetry. No message, browser permission prompt, polling, API call or provider request is made.
- Comments & reviews is a distinct work area for moderation and explicitly selected testimonials. Its old generic Inbox title made actual Notifications difficult to discover.
