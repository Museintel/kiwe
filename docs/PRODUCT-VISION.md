## The promise

Give a small business a beautiful, fast website it can confidently manage from a phone, and give its designer a repeatable path from client onboarding to launch and ongoing care. Keep the low entry cost, familiar content ownership, agency handoff and hosting choices that make WordPress useful.

Kiwe connects four experiences: the client describes the business, AI and a designer turn that context into a design, Seam makes that design editable and dynamic in WordPress, and DSA gives visitors and staff a coherent app experience. The product succeeds when clients stay involved and ordinary changes no longer require waiting for a freelancer.

## Design Context is the central product

The client workspace is the continuing relationship with the client, starting before a website exists. Identity, logo, tagline, story, audience, goals, brand, services, team, resources and applicable store settings should be understandable, editable and connected to the actual website.

Design Context means the entire task-based Administrator experience, not only the onboarding form or exported document. A client should find category renaming, post movement, team selection and eventual navigation/indexing choices beside the record they are managing. Services remain an optional configured integration, but the client Home task is always visible: adding a service joins it to Design Context without an enablement pre-step. An unconnected plan's category names are design intent; connected service taxonomies are native WordPress terms managed alongside post and product categories. Native labels and intentionally customized menu text need distinct treatment so routine edits preserve the designer's choices.

The intended journey is:

1. The designer establishes site ownership and invites the client into the business workspace.
2. The client supplies or corrects business facts, assets and preferences; missing information is visible and save progress is clear.
3. A bounded Design Context/SiteGraph export describes approved facts and available content. Private business or customer information is excluded unless an explicitly scoped workflow needs it.
4. AI proposes HTML/CSS/JS using that evidence. The client and designer review the design.
5. Seam converts the approved source into native Bricks templates. Explicit bindings connect text, media and repeated collections to real records.
6. The site is previewed and verified, then deployed with recovery evidence.
7. The client changes a native fact once and the bound website updates. A redesign can reuse that same business context.

Existing native WordPress/WooCommerce records remain authoritative. Design Context is an editing and planning surface, not a duplicate database. A present-day static heading will not become dynamic merely because it resembles a context field: binding evidence and post-import verification are required.

Future work extends this journey to a reviewed generate-site action and bounded frontend section/query choices. Those actions must retain the same context provenance, preview, permission and rollback boundaries. A designer remains useful for art direction, integrations and exceptional workflows.

## Two app experiences

**Visitor DSA:** one responsive surface for identity, account, search, saved items, navigation, notifications and optional commerce. A dock is one launcher; ordinary theme or Bricks elements can launch it through stable attributes. PWA installation, touch behavior, keyboard/focus handling, history and reduced motion are part of the experience. Server-rendered pages, advertising, consent and popup integrations still need to work. Shipping an attribute capability does not justify loading every module on every page.

Transitions should acknowledge an action immediately while useful work happens. A tip or game can make a real wait enjoyable, but must never artificially delay a ready destination or interfere with account recovery, payments or checkout. Basic browsing must survive JavaScript failure.

**Client workspace:** a dedicated application backed by the existing WordPress/WooCommerce system. Colorful, varied-size tiles form its primary navigation, inspired by Windows 8 and Nokia Lumia. There is no permanent WordPress sidebar or native admin toolbar in the finished client shell. Tiles open complete tasks such as updating a product, changing a logo, reviewing an authorized order or completing onboarding within the same app. React is the selected client-shell approach; measured speed and task completion remain the acceptance criteria. Forms need readable grouping, large touch controls, clear saving/error states and small-screen tables. The visual shell must support native editors, accessibility and third-party business tools rather than hiding broken integrations under attractive cards.

Kiwe's singleton `kiwe_super_admin` is currently the technical owner role; `administrator` is the client role only after the explicit ownership policy is activated. On a single site this is a Kiwe role, distinct from WordPress Multisite's network Super Admin. The founder's agency model starts with the designer holding technical ownership, with explicit transfer and recoverability. Menus never constitute authorization.

DSA and the client workspace should share one recognizable app language while respecting distinct data, permission and asset-loading boundaries. Reactive tasks may use native REST APIs within the site; separately hosted administration is optional. Beauty and ease of use need complete task interactions as well as responsive styling. See [App Experience Architecture](APP-EXPERIENCE-ARCHITECTURE.md) for accepted direction, implementation sequence and measured acceptance gaps.

## Identity, engagement and business modules

Key provides low-friction entry and branded delivery through an agency or client's WhatsApp connection while WordPress owns each site's accounts and sessions. Transactional messages and opted-in marketing serve different purposes; account creation alone must not silently enroll a person in cross-site marketing.

The durable notification system serves visitors and staff with audience-specific topics. Kiwe now has shared per-topic preferences, a bounded per-user inbox with cross-device acknowledgement/history, role-derived staff events, consent-aware client campaigns and category-aware first-publication alerts. Browser/PWA push, email, WhatsApp and SMS are delivery choices around that authority; unavailable providers remain visibly unavailable. Anonymous visitors can receive only exact-device PWA delivery, while registered contacts may use the channels they selected. Order status, abandoned carts, Guest/editorial work, security incidents and publisher alerts keep distinct audience rules while sharing the same credential-blind transports. Future kitchen or vertical events must add an explicit topic and permission boundary rather than creating another subscriber store.

Future modules include POS, bed booking, table booking, memberships, attendance and LMS. They should share identity, capabilities, context, events and UI components while using an established WordPress/WooCommerce integration where it already owns the required business state. Scheduling, capacity, payments and attendance require domain rules and real concurrency tests; a common UI does not make them interchangeable.

Future games could connect restaurant tables, award bounded coupons, and show opt-in profiles and scores across Kiwe sites. Future Key SSO and an entrepreneur network are separate architecture projects: local identity must not quietly become a global tracking identifier. Federation, consent, account linking/unlinking, moderation, abuse controls and tenant separation need explicit designs before launch.

## Engineering principles

- Shared hosting is the default deployment target for the WordPress component. No required Node server, Redis, daemon or paid AI call on a visitor request.
- Keep WordPress and WooCommerce authority; add reusable adapters rather than parallel content, user or order stores.
- Load optional capabilities when enabled and needed. Heavy conversion stays in Seam; expensive analysis and delivery leave interactive request paths.
- Prefer stable HTML, CSS, structured context and versioned bindings so sites remain transferable. Moving to a VPS is a hosting change; moving to React is a separately tested adapter/migration, not a promised one-click export today.
- AI assists with design and bounded improvements. Permissions, validation, credentials, financial state and publish authority remain deterministic.
- Optimize measured user outcomes. No invented security badges, guaranteed search rankings or unsupported speed claims.

SEO assistance can improve metadata, semantic structure, links, accessibility, indexing controls and content quality. A perfect internal checklist score is not a promise of search position, traffic or sales. We measure search visibility and business results separately.

## Success measures

Track time from onboarding invitation to complete usable context, first design review and verified launch; client completion of common mobile tasks; repeat redesign effort; and support requests per site. Performance measurements must separate Kiwe's incremental cost from the theme, media, advertisements, third-party plugins and hosting. A beautiful demo, a green source test or a high compiler confidence score alone cannot establish product readiness.
