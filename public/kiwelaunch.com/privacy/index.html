Enquiry privacy

When you ask for a demo or register partner interest, Kiwe Launch collects the details you submit so we can respond to that request.

What we collect

The forms ask for your name, email, business name, and any phone number or message you choose to share. We keep a short-lived record to limit repeated and automated submissions.

Who can see it

The information is stored on our hosting in a private Kiwe Launch inbox. Authorised Kiwe Launch operators use it to follow up on demo requests or partner interest.

Questions about your information

Use the contact form and say that your enquiry is about privacy. Include the email address you used for the original request.
