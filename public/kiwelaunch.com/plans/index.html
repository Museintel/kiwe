Choose a Kiwe plan

Launch your own website or manage a separate website for each client. Both plans are designed for monthly billing.

Client

For a business owner launching and managing one website.

Managed hosting included
  • Set up WordPress and Kiwe from your own Start dashboard.
  • Connect a domain you own or choose a new domain during setup.
  • Use the included hosting or connect an existing WordPress site.
  • Add your business details and review each design before it goes live.

Agency

For teams designing and managing websites for clients.

Managed hosting optional
  • Manage your website and client projects from an agency dashboard.
  • Choose managed hosting or connect a WordPress site you already host.
  • Give each client independent access to their own Start dashboard.
  • Use an agency WhatsApp number by default and choose a client number for individual sites.

These plans are a preview. Pricing and enrollment details will be published before monthly billing starts.
